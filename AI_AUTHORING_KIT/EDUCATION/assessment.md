# Assessment

`quizzes[]` + mission `objective.quiz`.

Rubric (runtime):
- Stars: 1 per required mission
- XP: sum of rewards minus nothing (hints do not subtract XP in v0.3)
- Rank: Novato <150, Aprendiz <400, Explorador <800, Maestro
- Completion: all `required` missions done

Feedback: `success` / `failure` strings on the mission. Hints via HUD.
