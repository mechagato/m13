# Lesson format

`education` metadata + `missions` body.

Suggested Bloom verbs in `objectives`:
- remember: identificar, nombrar
- understand: explicar, relacionar
- apply: usar, recorrer
- analyze: comparar
- evaluate: juzgar (quiz)
- create: not in v0.3 gameplay (no building)

`durationMin` 8–25. `grade` free text (`secundaria`, `preparatoria`).
